About us

We are a business dedicated to providing accessible programming and coding education for both youth and adults, with a focus on thinking creatively and outside the box.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals to use their technical skills to make a positive impact on the world around them.

Our vision

A more inclusive and welcoming environment in the tech industry.

Our history

Tech can be intimidating to some but as a new business, we aim to break that misconception, to teach how these valuable skills can help everyone.

Our process

We set out to create a space where anyone, regardless of background or experience level, could learn and thrive.

Our best service

We offer a range of courses, workshops, and mentorship opportunities to help our community members develop their technical skills and build their confidence.

Scratch Jr (6-8)

Scratch Jr is a kid-friendly programming language for creating interactive stories, games, and animations that fosters critical thinking, problem-solving, computational thinking, and creativity skills.

Scratch (8-13)

Scratch is a user-friendly visual programming language and platform developed by MIT that promotes creativity and problem-solving through interactive storytelling, gaming, and animations.

Coding with Roblox (10+)

The Coding with Roblox Lua curriculum teaches the basics of coding through creating games on the Roblox platform, including variables, loops, and conditionals. Students will learn problem-solving skills and gain experience in game design, while developing creativity and critical thinking abilities.

Intro to Web Development (13+)

The Introduction to Web Development curriculum covers the basic concepts of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including creating a simple webpage, adding interactivity, and styling elements. Students will apply these skills in a final project, where they create a website about themselves with guidance from the instructor.
